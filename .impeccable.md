# Silk & Velvet Events — Design Context

> Locked 2026-04-29. Source of truth for any design work in this repo.
> Update by editing this file directly, not by re-running `/impeccable teach`.

## Users

**Primary:** Engaged couples (25–45) in NYC, Long Island, and New Jersey researching wedding planners. They're stressed, decision-fatigued, comparing 3–5 options at once, on mobile during evening "browse mode" and on desktop during weekend "decide mode." They want to feel like they're in confident hands.

**Secondary:** Event hosts planning birthdays, baby showers, anniversaries, bachelorettes, and corporate gatherings — same NY/NJ/LI service area, often shorter lead times (4–8 weeks).

**Tertiary:** Visitors who only want balloon decor — they're comparing on visual aesthetic and price, often cross-referencing Instagram before clicking through.

**Job to be done:** "Show me you can pull this off without me having to micromanage you, and tell me what it costs."

## Brand Personality

Three words: **considered · warm · feminine-luxe**

Not "rustic." Not "boho." Not "corporate event firm." Sofiya's brand sits in a romantic-luxe lane — burgundy-and-gold wedding-stationery energy with enough modern restraint that it doesn't look like a 2010 wedding blog.

Voice (per `clients/silk-and-velvet-events/CLAUDE.md` and brand-voice rule): casual but professional, lead with outcomes, not features. "Where creativity meets elegance, making your vision unforgettable." Don't be precious — Sofiya is a working planner with two assistants and a real business, not a vibes-first creative.

## Aesthetic Direction

**Theme:** Light only. Wedding/event audience plans during daytime browsing on couches and at kitchen tables. Dark mode would feel wrong for the category.

**Typography (per Sofiya's Canva brand kit):**

- **Display script:** Halimun (confirmed 2026-05-04 by Sofiya, letter-by-letter) — script in the logo wordmark, for the brand wordmark and selective decorative accents only. NOT for body. Currently using fallback chain `'Halimun', 'Pinyon Script', cursive` until we have the file.
- **Body sans:** Glacial Indifference — for body, navigation, UI. Free font. Self-host once design is locked. Currently using fallback `'Glacial Indifference', system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif`.

## Design Principles

1. **Photography is the hero. Type frames it.** Sofiya's portfolio is the product. Layouts must give photos breathing room and resist the urge to stack icon-and-heading cards.
2. **Burgundy and gold are spent sparingly.** Cream and pink dominate (60–70% of any view). Burgundy is for important type and CTAs only. Gold is for rare moments. The 60-30-10 rule applies — accents work because they're rare.
3. **Real photos, real prices, real people.** Sofiya's bio, Angie and Inna's names, the actual $4,000-from price for full-service. Not "from $$$" or stock smiles. Trust comes from specificity.
4. **No iframes, no Canva embeds, no "click to expand" friction.** This site exists because the Canva version makes the form invisible and the gallery slow. Every interaction must be one tap closer than the original.
5. **Mobile is not an afterthought.** Most evening browse traffic is phone. The Contact widget's deep links (sms, wa.me, ig://, m.me) are the killer feature on mobile. Build them right.

## Anti-References

What this should explicitly NOT look like:
- Generic Squarespace wedding template (centered cream blocks, tasteful but interchangeable, "Plan Your Dream Day" hero)
- Etsy-rustic / barn-wedding (mason jars, kraft paper, hand-lettered "Mr. & Mrs.")
- Modern tech-startup ported to wedding industry (gradients, glass cards, sans everywhere)
- Wedding blog from 2014 (sidebar, narrow content column, Google Fonts Lora)

## Constraints

- **Static site.** No CMS in v1 — content is markdown files committed to git. Oscar pushes updates when Sofiya texts content.
- **Astro + Cloudflare Pages.** Already in Oscar's stack.
- **Form: Formspree.** Submissions land in Sofiya's gmail (or Workspace inbox once provisioned).
- **No JS frameworks.** Astro renders to plain HTML. JS is for the Contact widget and gallery lightbox only.
- **Performance:** Lighthouse ≥95 mobile. Photos lazy-loaded, AVIF/WebP, type subset.
- **Accessibility:** WCAG 2.1 AA. All burgundy-on-cream pairs meet 4.5:1. Reduced-motion respected.

## Three Aesthetic Variants in `/mockups/`

1. **`variant-a-editorial/`** — Editorial wedding magazine. Massive Noto Serif Display in italic at hero, asymmetric 12-column grids, full-bleed photo blocks, gold rule lines, generous whitespace. Quiet luxury.
2. **`variant-b-modern-soft/`** — Modern soft minimal. Glacial Indifference leads, serif only for accents, dusty-pink-tinted surfaces, balanced grids, pill buttons. Contemporary and approachable.
3. **`variant-c-old-world/`** — Old-world romantic. Halimun script leads, cream-paper background, hand-drawn ornamental dividers, organic asymmetry, vintage stationery feel. Most traditional/feminine.

All three use the same locked palette and same three fonts in different proportions. Pick by feel.
