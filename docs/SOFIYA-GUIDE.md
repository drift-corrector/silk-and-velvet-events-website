# Editing your website

You can change the words and photos on your site yourself, any time, from a
laptop or your phone. Nothing you do here can permanently break anything.

## Getting in

1. Go to **silkandvelvetevents.com/admin**
2. Click **Sign In with GitHub**

That's it. GitHub is just the login — you'll never need to look at it again.
Bookmark the `/admin` page so you don't have to type it each time.

## Making a change

Every change works the same way:

1. Click what you want to change in the left-hand menu
2. Edit it
3. Click **Save**

Your change goes live on the real site in about a minute. Refresh the page to
see it.

## Adding photos

This is the one you'll use most.

1. Click **Photos & Gallery** in the left menu
2. Scroll to **Photos** and click **Add photo**
3. Click the **Photo** box and pick your image
4. Fill in:
   - **Describe the photo** — what's in it, in a few words. Blind visitors' screen
     readers read this out, and Google uses it to understand the picture. Write
     something real, like "Blush and gold tablescape at a Long Island wedding" —
     not "photo1".
   - **Caption** — the little label that appears when someone hovers over it.
   - **Category** — which gallery page it belongs to.
   - **Show on the homepage** — turn on for your best work. The homepage shows
     the first six of these.
5. Click **Save**

Photos straight from your phone are fine. They get shrunk and optimised
automatically before they're uploaded, so you don't need to resize anything
first.

**To reorder photos**, drag them by the handle on the left. The order here is
the order they appear on the site.

**To remove a photo**, click the three dots on its row and choose Remove.

## Changing words on a page

Click **Page Text**, then the page you want.

Every page has the same few boxes:

- **Heading** — the big line at the top
- **Sub-heading** — the smaller line under it
- **Small label** — the little word above the heading, like "Services"
- **Browser tab / Google title** and **Google description** — what shows up in
  Google search results and in the browser tab. Worth keeping accurate; not
  worth agonising over.

### The fancy script text

On the headings, words wrapped in `<em>` and `</em>` appear in the gold script
font. So:

```
Three planners. <em>One promise.</em>
```

shows "Three planners." in the normal font and "One promise." in script.

Keep the `<em>` and `</em>` as a matched pair around whatever you want in
script. If it looks wrong, undo it and save again.

## Changing your phone number, email or Instagram

Click **Contact Details**. Change it once here and it updates everywhere on the
site — footer, contact page, FAQ, and the information Google shows about your
business.

## The rest of the menu

- **Services** — the individual services, their prices and what's included
- **Service Packages** — the five bundles on the services page
- **Questions & Answers** — the FAQ page
- **Reviews** — client reviews
- **How It Works** — the four process steps
- **Team** — you, Angie and Inna, including your photos and bios

## If something looks wrong

Tell Oscar. Every change is saved with a full history, so anything can be put
back exactly as it was. You cannot lose work and you cannot break the site
permanently — the worst case is that it looks odd for a few minutes.

## A few honest warnings

- **Don't change a category's "Web address"** unless you mean to. If you've
  shared a link to a gallery page, changing this breaks that link.
- **Saving takes a minute to show up.** If you don't see your change straight
  away, wait a minute and refresh before changing it again.
- **Don't edit the same thing on two devices at once.** Finish on one, then
  switch.
